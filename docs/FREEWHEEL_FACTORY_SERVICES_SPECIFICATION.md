## 🚲 1. Core Philosophy & System Architecture

Instead of simple "Basic / Full Service" tiers, **FREEWHEEL FACTORY** operates as an international-standard bicycle service, fitting, and engineering laboratory. This modular structure supports:
1. **Dynamic Pricing & Quotation** based on component level, bike type (Road, XC/MTB, Gravel, E-Bike, TT), and labor.
2. **Technician Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)** with checklists for quality control.
3. **Digital Bicycle Passport & Service History** that tracks component wear, torque specs, and historical service reports.

## 🛠️ 3. Detailed Breakdown of All 20 Service Categories

### 1. 🔧 General Bicycle Service
* **Target Audience:** Daily commuters, recreational riders, hybrids, entry-to-mid MTBs and road bikes.
* **Service Scope:**
  - Complete multi-point bicycle visual inspection
  - Fastener torque inspection to manufacturer specs
  - Front and rear brake adjustments & pad alignment
  - Front and rear derailleur indexing & cable tension tuning
  - Chain cleaning, degreasing & precision lubrication
  - Drivetrain surface cleaning
  - Lateral wheel alignment check
  - Tyre pressure check and inflation to rider/terrain specs
  - Headset, bottom bracket, and hub play inspection
  - Comprehensive safety check & technician test ride

---

### 2. 🛠️ Premium / Complete Service
* **Target Audience:** Enthusiasts, high-mileage riders, quarterly/bi-annual overhauls.
* **Service Scope:**
  - *Includes all General Service tasks PLUS:*
  - Full drivetrain removal (crankset, chain, cassette, derailleurs)
  - Deep ultrasonic cleaning of all compatible drivetrain components
  - Chain wear elongation measurement (% stretch gauge)
  - Detailed cassette and chainring tooth profile wear assessment
  - Complete brake system inspection & caliper piston reset
  - Inner cables & outer housings wear/friction inspection
  - Precision wheel truing in professional truing stand with spoke tension check
  - Hub, headset, and bottom bracket bearing smoothness inspection
  - Complete frame and fork clean & polish
  - Digital service report generated and uploaded to Customer Passport

---

### 3. 🏁 Performance / Race Service
* **Target Audience:** Cross-Country (XC), Enduro, Road racers, Triathletes, Gran Fondo riders.
* **Service Scope:**
  - Complete race-chassis deep inspection
  - Drivetrain frictional efficiency check (waxing / low-friction lube application)
  - Brake system modulation and bite-point verification
  - Spoke tension balance & wheel deflection testing
  - Suspension sag, high/low-speed compression & rebound optimization
  - Tyre pressure micro-tuning (dry/wet, rim width, tyre casing matching)
  - Cockpit angle, reach, brake lever ergonomics check
  - Cleat position and alignment verification on cycling shoes
  - Pre-race safety pass certification & post-service high-intensity test ride
  - Digital Race-Preparation Certificate issued

---

### 4. 🚵 Suspension Service
* **4.1 Basic Suspension Service:**
  - External stanchion clean, wiper lip inspection, travel O-ring replacement, air pressure & sag check.
* **4.2 Lower-Leg Fork Service (50-hour):**
  - Lower-leg removal, bath oil draining, internal cleaning, foam ring wash/saturation, wiper seal replacement, correct weight bath oil injection, reassembly and torque verification.
* **4.3 Full Fork Service (200-hour):**
  - Complete tear-down, damper cartridge teardown & bleeding, air spring rebuild, O-ring/seal kit replacement, damper oil replacement, IFP pressure charging, dynamic dyno test.
* **4.4 Rear Shock Air-Can & Full Service:**
  - Air-can removal, seal kit replacement, damper oil service, nitrogen/air chamber recharge, rebound/lockout function verification.

---

### 5. ⚙️ Drivetrain Service
* **Service Scope:**
  - Chain cleaning & digital wear percentage measurement (0.5% / 0.75% wear alerts)
  - Chain replacement & quick-link installation
  - Cassette removal, ultrasonic degrease, inspection, replacement
  - Chainring wear diagnosis, spider bolt torque verification, replacement
  - Crankset removal, spindle inspection, bottom bracket replacement (threaded / pressfit)
  - Derailleur hanger precision laser/gauge alignment
  - Mechanical cable and housing replacement with low-friction liners
  - Electronic shifting (Shimano Di2, SRAM AXS, Campagnolo EPS) diagnostic scanning, firmware upgrade, micro-trim tuning
  - Hot-melt chain waxing / ultrasonic immersion bath option

---

### 6. 🛑 Brake Service
* **6.1 Mechanical Disc & Rim Brakes:**
  - Cable/housing renewal, caliper arm pivot lube, pad wear check, rotor trueing, pad-to-rotor gap alignment.
* **6.2 Hydraulic Disc Brakes:**
  - Pad thickness measurement (min 0.5mm backing plate safety rule), rotor thickness micrometer check.
  - Caliper piston deep cleaning, lubrication, and equalized piston advancement.
  - Hydraulic line replacement, olive & barb fitting insertion, stealth routing.
  - Complete system bleed (Shimano/Magura Mineral Oil, SRAM/Avid DOT 5.1).
  - High-load brake performance and heat fade inspection.

---

### 7. 🛞 Wheel & Hub Service
* **7.1 Wheel Truing & Tension Balancing:**
  - Radial and lateral truing, dishing gauge centering, digital tensiometer spoke balance.
* **7.2 Hub Overhaul:**
  - Cup-and-cone loose ball clean, inspection, repack with waterproof grease.
  - Sealed cartridge bearing extraction and press installation with bearing drifts.
  - Freehub pawl / ratchet ring inspection, spring replacement, freehub light lube application.
* **7.3 Custom Wheel Building (Master Wheel Lab):**
  - Rider weight, discipline, rim ERD, hub flange pitch circle diameter calculations.
  - Spoke calculation (straight pull / J-bend), 2x/3x lacing pattern, brass/alloy nipples.
  - Multi-stage stress-relieving, uniform spoke tensioning, certificate of build tolerances.

---

### 8. 🛞 Tyre & Tubeless Service
* **Service Scope:**
  - Inner tube replacement & high-pressure rim tape inspection
  - Tyre casing inspection for cuts, sidewall damage, bead integrity
  - Tubeless conversion (tubeless rim tape application, valve installation, tyre mounting)
  - Sealant replenishment & old sealant booger removal
  - Tyre leak diagnosis (submersion dunk tank test & bead reseat)
  - XC / Trail Tyre Pressure Optimisation Calculator based on rider mass, rim inner width, tyre volume, and terrain.

---

### 9. 📐 Professional 3D Bike Fitting (*Signature Offering*)
* **Stage 1 — Rider Biomechanical Assessment:**
  - Physical interview (injury history, riding discipline, flexibility, pelvic tilt, leg length discrepancy).
* **Stage 2 — Real-Time 3D Motion Analysis:**
  - Dynamic infrared/optical markers tracking knee extension angle (35-40 deg), ankle articulation, hip angle, torso angle, shoulder extension.
* **Stage 3 — Ergonomic Adjustments:**
  - Millimeter-accurate saddle height, fore/aft setback, saddle tilt.
  - Cockpit adjustment (stem length, handlebar angle, hood rotation, reach/drop).
  - Insole arch support & cleat wedging.
* **Stage 4 — Verification & Delivery:**
  - Comprehensive before/after digital report with dynamic 3D fit coordinates.

---

### 10. 👟 Cleat & Foot-Bed Fitting
* **Service Scope:**
  - Foot arch profile and longitudinal arch measurement
  - 1st and 5th metatarsal head alignment on pedal spindle axis
  - Cleat rotational angle matching natural foot flare / tibial rotation
  - Medial / lateral stance width (Q-Factor) adjustment
  - Left vs. right leg differential compensation (shims / varus-valgus wedges)
  - Final torque verification of 3-bolt (Look/SPD-SL) and 2-bolt (SPD/Crankbrothers) setups

---

### 11. 🔩 Bearing Service
* **Service Scope:**
  - Headset upper/lower cartridge bearings clean, grease, or press-out replacement.
  - Bottom bracket bearings (BSA, BB30, PF30, BB86/92, T47) extraction and precision press.
  - Full-suspension pivot & linkage cartridge bearings extraction, inspection for binding/play, replacement with Enduro MAX full-complement bearings.
  - Pedal spindle overhaul & bushing/bearing refresh.

---

### 12. 🧰 Component Installation & Upgrades
* **Service Scope:**
  - Customer-provided components installed with full warranty on workmanship.
  - Groupset installation (drivetrain, shifters, crank, derailleurs, chain, cassette).
  - Cockpit installation (carbon handlebars, integrated bar-stem combos, internal cable routing).
  - Dropper post installation (stealth internal cable/hydraulic routing, lever positioning).
  - Fork and shock upgrades (steerer cutting, star nut installation, crown race press).

---

### 13. ⚡ E-Bike Service & Diagnostics
* **Mechanical:** Heavy-duty e-bike rated brake pads, e-bike chain & cassette wear, hub motor spoke tensioning, reinforced tyre mounting.
* **Electrical & Diagnostics:**
  - Battery capacity and health diagnostics (% state of health).
  - Diagnostic scan for error codes (Bosch, Shimano STEPS, Bafang, Brose, etc.).
  - Speed sensor alignment and wiring harness inspection.
  - Firmware updating & display console troubleshooting.

---

### 14. 🚴 Bike Assembly & New Bike Setup (*PRO ASSEMBLY*)
* **Service Scope:**
  - Unboxing & full frame inspection for freight defects/scratches.
  - Headset & bottom bracket grease check.
  - Complete component installation and torque check with calibrated digital torque wrenches.
  - Wheel true & spoke tension balance out of the box.
  - Full brake bleed / adjustment & gear indexing.
  - Initial cockpit, saddle, and tyre pressure setup.
  - Digital Assembly QC Certificate with stamped serial number.

---

### 15. 🏆 Race Preparation Service (*RACE READY PASS*)
* **Timing:** Conducted 24–48 hours prior to race/event.
* **Checklist:**
  - 40-point safety and torque check
  - Braking power & pad life check (>60% required for race pass)
  - Drivetrain ultrasonic clean + race lubrication/wax
  - Tubeless sealant top-up & pressure check
  - Suspension lockouts & damper switches tested under load
  - Issue official **RACE READY — PASS** certificate or urgent remedial advisory.

---

### 16. 🚨 Emergency, Doorstep & Roadside Rescue
* **Doorstep Van Service:** Mobile workshop equipped with workstands, truing stands, ultrasonic tanks, compressors, and common spares.
* **Roadside Emergency Support:** On-call flat tyre fix, broken chain splicing, emergency hanger straightening, gear cable replacement.
* **Event Neutral Support:** Paddock service at major cycling races and brevets.

---

### 17. 📋 Bicycle Health Inspection & Digital Passport
* **Visual Status Indicators:**
  - 🟢 **GOOD** (Within operational tolerance, no immediate action needed)
  - 🟡 **ATTENTION REQUIRED** (Showing wear, schedule service in next 30-60 days)
  - 🔴 **REPLACE / REPAIR** (Safety hazard / out of tolerance, immediate replacement needed)
* **Components Evaluated:** Frame, Fork, Shock, Wheels, Drivetrain, Brakes, Bearings, Cockpit.
* **Output:** Digital Bicycle Passport updated in cloud database with unique Bike ID / QR code.

---

### 18. 🚲 Pre-Owned Bicycle Inspection (Used Bike Authority)
* **Target Audience:** Buyers/sellers of high-value bicycles (₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000+).
* **Inspection Protocol:**
  - Carbon frame ultrasound/tap test for hidden delamination & structural cracks.
  - Stanchion scratch and damper internal check.
  - Wheel rim brake-track or disc rotor wear micrometer measurement.
  - Component originality verification vs. stock factory spec.
  - Stolen bike registry check / Frame serial number verification.
  - Fair market valuation & estimated immediate repair cost assessment report.

---

### 19. 🧪 Advanced Diagnostic Services
* **Diagnostic Capabilities:**
  - Digital tensiometer wheel analysis reports.
  - Electronic shifting diagnostics (battery health, shifter actuation logs).
  - Creak & unwanted noise acoustic triangulation and isolation.
  - Thread repair, chasing, facing (bottom bracket shells, disc brake mounts, steerer tubes).

---

### 20. 🧼 Bicycle Detailing & Spa
* **Level 1 — Express Wash:** pH-neutral biodegradable foam wash, wheel clean, drivetrain wipe, express lube.
* **Level 2 — Deep Detail:** Full drivetrain degreasing, cassette & chain deep bath, frame polish, tyre dressing.
* **Level 3 — Showroom Ceramic Detail:** Full disassembly detail, paint clay bar decontamination, ceramic coating application for matte/gloss carbon/alloy frames, anti-stick mud repellent treatment.
