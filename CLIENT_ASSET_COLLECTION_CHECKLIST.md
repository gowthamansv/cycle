## Section A: Images & Photography

### Photography Tips for Best Results
- **Lighting:** Shoot during the day with bright, even workshop lighting (avoid dark shadows or harsh flash).
- **Clean Background:** Tidy the workstand, tool wall, and floor before taking photos.
- **Orientation:**
  - **Landscape (Horizontal)** — hold your phone/camera sideways for banners, shop views, bikes, and service cards.
  - **Square / Portrait (Vertical)** — ideal for individual staff headshots.
- **Resolution:** Modern smartphone cameras in original quality (at least `1600 px` wide) work great if well-lit and sharp.
